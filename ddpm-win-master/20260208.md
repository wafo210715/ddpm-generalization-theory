- **Logarithmic checkpointing**: Saves at 100, 250, 500, 1000, 2500, 5000, 10000, etc.

## Training Configuration Insights

### Current Config (MNIST)
- **Model size**: 64 channels (~half of research repos which use 128)
- **Image size**: 32×32 (MNIST is 28×28 native, resized for CNN)
- **Batch size**: 128
- **Steps**: 50,000 (potentially excessive for MNIST)
- **GPU memory**: ~2.2 GB per GPU peak (test), ~6.8 GB observed in training

### Research Repository Comparison
| Repo | Base Channels | Max Channels |
|------|---------------|--------------|
| Why-Diffusion-Models-Don-t-Memorize | 128 | 1024 |
| EDM (DDPM++) | 128 | 256 |
| EDM (NCSN++) | 192 | 768 |

## Training Time Estimates

- **50,000 steps**: ~3.5 hours on dual RTX 3090s
