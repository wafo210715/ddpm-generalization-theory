### 3. Cosine Schedule Issue Identified and Resolved
**Problem**: Cosine schedule produced black images during sampling
- **Root cause**: Beta values approached 1.0 at late timesteps (t > 900), causing division by zero in reverse diffusion
- **Analysis**: `beta = 1 - (alpha_bar[t] / alpha_bar[t-1])` becomes unstable when alpha_bar drops rapidly
- **Resolution**: Removed cosine schedule entirely from codebase (not used by either research repo)

## Lessons Learned

1. **Cosine Schedule Instability**: The cosine schedule formula produces beta values approaching 1.0 at late timesteps, causing sampling failure. This is a known issue with the discrete-time implementation.

2. **Path Issues**: Relative paths in Python scripts can save files to unexpected locations. Always use `os.path.dirname(os.path.abspath(__file__))` for script-relative paths.

3. **Parallel Training Strategy**: Running two schedules on separate GPUs simultaneously (parallel single-GPU) is faster than sequential dual-GPU training (70 min vs 80 min).

4. **Research Repo Alignment**: Neither of our primary research repos (Why-Diffusion-Models-Don-t-Memorize, Diffusion-Model-Generalizability) use cosine schedule. Removing it keeps our codebase aligned with our research focus.
