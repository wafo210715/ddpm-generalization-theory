what each shuffle costs

Clumping is Moran's I: does a unit resemble the ones next to it. Shuffling drops it to zero, because it is the only column here that is about where. Agreement with the other sheets is how well this sheet's values line up with the other five at the same unit — shuffling destroys that too, and no per-sheet histogram would notice.
