What this does and does not license

Where imagery exists it is abundant and current. The median covered cell holds 1 535 images, 60 % are panoramic, and the median image in 1 678 of the 1 930 covered cells was taken in 2023 or later. For a perception layer that is a good position: enough frames per cell to sample from, and recent enough to describe the city as it now stands.

And it is absent from more of the built city than the earlier probe suggested. I reported before that 94 to 100 % of 150 m cells in several dense districts had imagery. That was true and it was six central districts. Measured across all 2 968 tiles holding a hundred buildings or more, 1 260 have no imagery at all — 42.5 % of the built city. Coverage correlates 0.64 with building count, which is well below what a layer describing the fabric would score.

So perception is buildable for the core and not for the whole study area, which is the same shape of problem as places. The options are the same too: restrict the perception channel to covered cells and say so, treat it as a cell condition where it exists, or accept a channel that is absent across two fifths of the built tiles and let the model see that absence as a value.
