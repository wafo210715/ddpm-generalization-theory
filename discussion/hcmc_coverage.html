Street view: no data points yet, and one thing is needed

We hold no street view imagery locations. Nothing has been fetched, there is no Mapillary credential on this machine, and the coverage claims made earlier in this project came from a probe run with Mapillary's own published demo token — fine for ninety read-only requests, not a basis for surveying five thousand tiles.

What is known so far is second-hand but specific: a ridesharing company put roughly 9 million images across 27 000 km of Hanoi and Ho Chi Minh City onto Mapillary from April 2023, shot on helmet cameras from scooters, and a sampled probe found imagery in 94 to 100 % of 150 m cells inside several dense alley districts. That is encouraging and it is not a coverage map.

  1. Register a free token at the Mapillary developer dashboard. It takes about two minutes, needs no payment and no approval step.
  2. Paste it here, or put it in the environment as MAPILLARY_TOKEN.
