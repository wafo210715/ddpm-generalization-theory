Why the numbers moved

Every sparsity result this project has recorded was a measurement of OpenStreetMap's completeness in Vietnam, not of Ho Chi Minh City. Swapping the base layer changes three things that sit upstream of the data work: the trainable tile count, the ring radius the diversity channel needs, and the validity of the building density channel already rasterized.
