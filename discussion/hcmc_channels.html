HCMC Layer Inspection

Sixteen indicator layers, computed on the real city and cut into the tile grid the model would train on. A cell is a stack of grids and a unit is one pixel inside it, so nothing here reduces a tile to a single number.

The city, one layer at a time

Every layer over the whole municipality. Switch between them to see that they are not versions of one pattern: the built core, the water, the green belt and the road network each own a different part of the city. This image is a rendering size, not a sampling unit. At the real 1.17 m unit the city would be about 580 000 pixels across, so the whole-city view is drawn at eight units per tile and the tile renders below carry the actual grain.

The built-form layers are measured differently here than below, and they have to be. At 150 m, asking "is a building under this point" samples a pixel centre, and a 60 m² building almost never contains it — done that way the city came out a 2 % speckle. So at this size footprints are coverage by area per cell, height is an area-weighted mean, and floor-area ratio follows from both. At the sampling unit below, where a building spans several pixels, the binary field is the right question and is what you see there.

Scale

0

Clipped at the 1st and 99th percentile of the units inside the municipality. Scaling on the bounding box instead blanks every sparse layer, because most of the box is not the city.

What a tile mean would throw away

The share of each layer's variance that lives inside a tile rather than between tiles, measured at the sampling unit over a sample of built-up tiles. A tile average keeps only the rest. For footprints it discards about nine tenths of the signal.

It also sorts the layers without anyone deciding in advance. The ones at the bottom barely vary inside a tile at all, so they belong attached to the cell as conditions rather than carried as grids — which is the plan's own three-way sort, settled by measurement.

One caution about this number. It saturates early: footprints reach about 85 % by eight units per tile and do not move from 128 up to 1024. Variance is fixed by how much of a thing there is, and downsampling preserves that. What finer units buy is arrangement, which variance cannot see, so this measure cannot decide between 128 and 512 px. That comparison needs a spatial statistic.

One tile, every layer

Densest tile first. Here the unit is real: individual plots and the alleys between them are legible, which is what the 1.17 m unit was chosen for. Each tile is computed with a 400 m buffer and then cropped, so a 100 m disc or a 300 m ring near the edge reads real neighbours instead of the edge of an array.

Distributions, per unit

Every pixel counted, pooled over sampled tiles, on a log1p axis so the zero spike and the tail are both visible. None of these is Gaussian and none needs to be. The reference papers' Gaussian mixtures are solvable toy models, not assumptions about data. What the shape decides is each layer's transform, and the number that matters is the zero fraction, because no monotone transform spreads an atom.

Correlation between layers, per unit

Spearman, on units inside the sampled tiles

The C1 baseline, the correlationist control, run on our own data. It is here to flag near-duplicate layers: anything close to ±1 is carrying information another layer already has.

What gets compared before and after training

One function, run on both sides. The same descriptor measured on real cells now, and on generated cells at every checkpoint.

Before — this page
  • B10 per-layer marginals, zero fraction, skew, tails. Chooses each transform. Gaussianity not required.
  • B10b the joint pattern of the layers together, kept as a distribution so it can be compared later.
  • B11 transform, then standardize each layer and store the parameters, so a sample can be unpacked.
  • C1 the correlationist baseline, and the flag for near-duplicate layers.
After — every checkpoint
  • E1 sample, then undo the standardization to get back to physical units.
  • E2 memorization as a k-NN gap ratio, against the untrained noise floor from D2.
  • E3 the same descriptor as B10b, on generated cells. What stays put across mutants and through the window is the structural signal.
  • E4 the window itself: structure onset to memorization onset.
