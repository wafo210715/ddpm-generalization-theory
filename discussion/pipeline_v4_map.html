Write the indicator specification table Written and decided 15 Sep. indicator_specification.md: thirteen grids, three conditions, 3 010 cells, 256 px first. Places stay grids with absence as a value at a 300 m ring; perception is a condition on covered cells only; Côn Đảo’s ten tiles are out. Four defects in the preview code are listed there as B8 requirements, and the junction rule is already patched.

Why

  • Each row fixes subject, source, formula, measurement scale, ring scale, and expected sparsity.
  • The row count sets the channel count, so this comes before any loader change.
A2action

Search for human-centric layers for HCMC

Why

  • Your seventh stage: joint form and activity, the ISM proposal's original wording.
  • VIIRS night lights is the one layer known to be available, but at 500 m it is nearly flat inside a 1.2 km tile. Treat coarse layers as cell-level conditions, or find building-scale activity data.
  • Decides whether activity enters this paper or the next. The model no longer forces the choice.
A3action

Move the raw HCMC vectors and the Python environment to the Linux box

Why

  • The 96 GB Pro 6000 is here. The OSM geopackages, grid, and venv are not.
  • The pilot's RGB cells need not come; the raw vectors must, since every channel is recomputed.
STAGE BBuild the datasetyour third
B1action

Cut the study area into zoom-15 map tiles (1,201 m)

Why

  • In generalization theory (Bonnaire et al.) the memorization onset grows linearly with sample count. Four times the tiles of zoom 14 widens the observable generalization window.
  • Typology is assigned per cell. 1.2 km cells are purer than 2.4 km cells, which keeps mutants clean.
  • Reversible: four zoom-15 tiles merge into one zoom-14 tile. Tile addressing standardizes other cities later.
  • Verified: 1,200 to 1,203 m across all of HCMC. No overlapping windows: near-duplicates corrupt the k-NN gap ratio.
B2action

Make the calculation unit equal to the pixel: 1024 px per tile, a 1.17 m unit

Why

  • The unit is where an indicator's value is read, not where it is measured. Measurement scale belongs to each indicator: its decay radius, its ring, the polygon itself. So the unit is free above a floor set by the finest indicator, the footprints.
  • Your call, given HCMC's ground conditions: at 1.17 m a 3 m alley is two to three pixels wide and a 5 m rowhouse keeps its outline. The pilot's 50 m units under 7.8 m pixels broke the definition.
  • Cost grows with pixel count, not meaning: 64× the pilot's compute per step, so a 500K-step run is weeks by naive scaling. Debug at 128 or 256, calibrate at 512, run the experiments at 1024. Smaller mutants memorize sooner and need fewer steps.
  • At this size the noise schedule must be shifted and the U-Net needs more levels; Simple Diffusion (Hoogeboom et al. 2023) trained 1024 px in pixel space exactly this way. Reasoning drawn in the companion page.
B3action

Buildings at 2 to 3 m: rasterize footprints, binary and density Read B3–B5 as history, 15 Sep. These three steps are one per channel of the pilot’s RGB file — B buildings, R accessibility, G diversity. The “six channels” elsewhere in this map is that triple with B and G split in two plus one activity layer, which is why they are named B1, B2, R, G1, G2, H. Six was never chosen on the merits. The layer set is now the “Have it” rows of the data landscape: 16 computable, 13 grids and 3 cell conditions, each with its own measurement scale. See the corrections page.

Why

  • Rowhouses are 4 to 6 m wide and alleys 2 to 4 m. Coarser units fuse them into blocks.
  • The pilot's 150 m buffer coverage ratio flattened them to a scalar: Moran's I near 0.
B4action

Accessibility: gravity model on the road network, decay of a few hundred metres

Why

  • Exponential decay yields a smooth field. Its meaning lives in the decay radius, so reading it at 2.35 m costs nothing and loses nothing.
  • It was the only pilot channel with spatial structure (Moran's I 0.25).
B5action

Diversity: Shannon entropy over a ring of a few hundred metres, plus POI count

Why

  • Entropy needs at least two POIs and two categories. At 50 m the pilot channel was 97.5 % zeros.
  • Count travels as its own channel, so diversity does not collapse into density.
  • Semantic categories only if coverage holds up; the checkpoint below decides. Settled 15 Sep: it holds on Overture, and entropy now runs over 11 functional classes in a 300 m ring. Qualified by A1 later that day: “holds” was measured on the 138 OSM-dense tiles. On the full urban set the 300 m ring is defined on 35 % of units, 94 % on the densest band; the ring stays at 300 m, absence is written as a value, and the core becomes a C7 mutant.
B6checkpoint

Checkpoint: POI-per-ring coverage report

Why

  • The fraction of units whose ring is non-degenerate must be high before any training. Ran, and reversed, 15 Sep: on OSM it failed — 1.1 % of units at a 50 m ring, which is what forced the ring out to 300 m, where the channel largely reports distance to the nearest mapped cluster. On Overture, over the same tiles, it is 57.0 % at 50 m and 79.7 % at 100 m. The sparsity was OSM’s, not the city’s, and the wide ring is no longer forced. Corrected again by A1, 15 Sep: those two figures are on the 138 tiles with ≥ 100 OSM buildings, not on the urban set. Over all 3 020 tiles with ≥ 100 Overture buildings the defined share is 6.3 % at 50 m, 13.4 % at 100 m and 35.3 % at 300 m (78.5 % on the 938 tiles above 1 000 buildings). The ring is decided at 300 m; see the corrections page, section 4, and A1.
  • You asked for this check explicitly.
B7action

Resample every channel to the common pixel grid (Liu et al. 2024)

Why

  • Each channel is computed at its natural scale first. Alignment happens only at the end.
  • Liu's image-to-image work fused independently rendered layers the same way.
B8action

Store cells as multi-channel tensors, not RGB PNGs

Why

  • The U-Net reads its channel count from the config; one channel or six both work. Only the PNG loader forces three.
  • No fusion step to defend, and activity layers become extra channels later.
  • Cost: no single picture of all channels, and no pretrained perceptual metric. FID and SSCD were already unusable on urban rasters.
B9action

Decide how multi-channel cells will be shown Answered 15 Sep. Small multiples won, and the conventions are settled in practice by the ward atlas (one panel per layer, one colour map per indicator, a fixed unit for the whole city, tile grid and boundary drawn on) and the coverage page (the tile grid itself as the canvas, log scale where density spans orders of magnitude). False-colour composites were not adopted: with sixteen layers, any choice of three is arbitrary.

Why

  • Sixteen greyscale grids cannot be one picture. Fix the conventions before the first sample exists: small multiples, one panel per channel; false-colour composites, any three channels to RGB, the remote-sensing habit; one perceptually uniform colour map per indicator.
  • Starting references, from memory, verify before citing: Tufte 1990, Envisioning Information, on small multiples; Crameri, Shephard and Heron 2020, Nature Communications, on colour maps; Munzner 2014, Visualization Analysis and Design, on encoding channels.
B10checkpoint

Checkpoint: per-channel marginals, zero fraction, skew, tails

Why

  • Chooses the transform for each channel.
  • Gaussianity is not required. The mixtures in the reference papers are solvable toy models, not data assumptions.
B10bcheckpoint

Checkpoint: the joint pattern of all layers together, as well as each one alone

Why

  • Not a replacement for B10. Each channel's distribution across the whole city is its own finding, and stays one: it is what says whether an indicator is sparse, skewed, or flat, and it is what chooses the transform.
  • What it adds: layers that each look right separately can still combine in ways the city never does, and nothing in a set of marginals can see that.
  • A unit's column of numbers is one point in a many-dimensional space. The real city occupies only part of that space: dense building with no shops is ordinary, high entropy with no POIs is impossible by construction. The occupied region is itself a structure the model can get right or wrong.
  • Same object as C3's clustering, which already treats a unit's column of scores as a point. This measures it as a distribution instead, so it can be compared against generated cells at E3.
  • Read it twice: in data space to say what the combinations mean, and in standardized space for anything using distance, since otherwise the widest channel decides which units are alike.
B11action

Transform, then standardize each channel; store the parameters

Why

  • A low-variance channel is pure noise over most of the diffusion trajectory. Standardization equalizes the signal-to-noise schedule across channels.
  • Sparse, heavy-tailed channels get log1p or a quantile map first.
  • The stored parameters are the decoder: unpacking a sample is just undoing this step.
STAGE CPre-training analysisyour fourth
C1action

Correlationist baseline: correlations among indicators across the study area

Why

  • The control condition the paper argues against, on the same data.
  • Not feature selection. The network handles correlated, non-linear inputs by itself.
  • Flags near-duplicate channels. Pilot diversity and accessibility correlated at 0.88.
C2action

Write the cell descriptor: one function for real and generated cells

Why

  • Per-channel mean, std, zero fraction, Moran's I, skewness; within-cell cross-channel correlation; unit-class composition.
  • Must be computable from the raster alone. Generated cells have no vectors behind them.
  • The pilot's statistics ran only on generated samples through separate code paths.
C3action

Typology: HDBSCAN on per-unit indicator vectors, then cell composition

Why

  • DBSCAN needs only a vector and a distance. A unit's column of scores is one point, never split into one-hots.
  • Never on raw full-resolution tensors, where distance is meaningless. HDBSCAN handles varying density.
  • Typologies are the subspaces of Wang et al. That unifies intrinsic dimension, minimal set, and mutants.
C4action

Intrinsic dimension: global PCA k95, Levina-Bickel MLE, PCA inside each typology

Why

  • PCA is linear and overestimates on curved manifolds. MLE follows curvature; the formula bug is fixed.
  • Wang et al. size the minimal set by per-subspace dimension.
  • The pilot's k95 of 33 was accessibility-dominated and must be re-estimated.
C4baction

Map the real dataset in two dimensions, coloured by typology

Why

  • PCA to about 50 numbers per cell first, then UMAP or t-SNE. One map for the whole stack and one per grid, fitted on real cells only.
  • Shows whether typologies separate and where the minimal set sits. It is the before picture, made before any model exists.
  • Use UMAP if generated cells must later land on this same map: UMAP can place new points on a fitted map, t-SNE cannot.
C5checkpoint

Checkpoint: does each typology sit in a low-dimensional subspace?

Why

  • The Wang et al. assumption is a union of low-dimensional subspaces, a joint geometry, not marginal normality.
  • If not, revisit the clustering before sizing any dataset. Mirrored later by the denoiser's Jacobian rank.
C6action

Select the minimal baseline set by an explicit rule

Why

  • PCA gives directions, not a subset. Use k-center greedy in PCA space or one representative per typology.
  • Sized per typology from C4.
  • Below the intrinsic-dimension threshold the model memorizes, so this set is the control where the generalization window should be short or absent.
C7action

List the dataset mutants: baseline, +A, +B, −A, class-balanced, full

Why

  • The one place where correlationist and connectionist views can disagree.
  • Invariants across mutants are the irreducible structure.
  • Each mutant gets its own model size and steps, because the memorization onset scales with n. Hypotheses H1 to H4 from June.
STAGE DTrainyour fifth
D1action

Train the DDPM directly on the cell tensors, with no latent space

Why

  • Noise is added to the indicator grids themselves, so what the model memorizes or generalizes is measured in the data, not in a black box. A learned VAE latent would move it there and impose natural-image priors.
  • "Pixel" here means unit, not colour. The tensor is C grids of unit scores; see the companion page.
  • Model size and steps per mutant follow from the memorization onset scaling with n. At 1024 px the noise schedule must be shifted and the U-Net needs more levels.
  • Runs on the Pro 6000 once A3 is done.
D2action

Score F_mem on an untrained model to get the metric's noise floor

Why

  • Random weights produce nonsense cells. Whatever memorization fraction they score is the floor; only values above it mean the trained model copied.
  • Independent of encoding, so it survives the redesign. Feeds the onset threshold in E4.
D3checkpoint

Checkpoint: locate the memorization onset on one short run first

Why

  • Every mutant is its own multi-day run, and the step budget only makes sense once tau_mem is known for this data. Guessing it wastes the budget in whichever direction the guess is wrong.
  • Measured on the Pro 6000: a 500K-step run at 1024 px is 4 to 8 days. Sizing four mutants from a guess risks a month.
  • Corrected 15 Sep: this read “the dataset is small. Of 1,881 zoom-15 tiles only 617 hold any building and 139 hold a hundred or more … so the window may open and close early here.” Measured on the current city and on Overture rather than OSM, it is 5,170 tiles, of which 4,845 hold a building and 3,020 hold a hundred or more. Since generalization theory has the onset growing with sample count, the trainable set being roughly twenty times larger means the window should be wider, and a step budget sized from the old figure will be too short. See the corrections page.
  • Run one mutant with logarithmic checkpoints until F_mem clears the D2 noise floor, then set every other run's budget from where that happened.
STAGE ESample and analyzeyour fifth and sixth
E1action

Sample every checkpoint; undo the standardization

Why

  • A sample is a tensor in data space, not an embedding.
  • Unpacking is the stored shift and scale in reverse, plus an optional three-grid rendering for humans.
E2action

Memorization: k-NN gap ratio on the standardized tensors

Why

  • Raw distance is dominated by the highest-variance channel. The pilot measured accessibility memorization.
  • The descriptor is too coarse to detect copying of a specific cell.
E3action

Distribution: descriptor on real vs generated, per mutant, per checkpoint

Why

  • Same function on both sides.
  • Irreducible characteristics: invariant across mutants and stable through the generalization window.
E3baction

Put generated cells on the real dataset's 2-D map, per grid and per checkpoint

Why

  • Same PCA and UMAP fitted in C4b, so real and generated points share one map. Where generated points fill, avoid, or pile onto real ones shows what each grid learned.
  • Per grid isolates which indicator's distribution drifted. Repeated over checkpoints it becomes a film of the generalization window.
  • Qualitative: distances on such a map are not measurements. The descriptor histograms and Wasserstein distances carry the numbers.
E3caction

Spatial relations between grids: real versus generated, before and after training

Why

  • Within a cell, how the grids sit relative to each other in space: bivariate Moran's I between two grids, co-location of unit classes, cross-correlograms by distance.
  • The pilot's synergy analysis compared cell-level averages, which cannot tell whether buildings sit on accessible units or merely share a cell with them.
  • Computed once on real cells and at every checkpoint on generated cells, so the change reads from what to what.
E4action

Locate the window per mutant: structure onset to memorization onset above the noise floor

Why

  • Structure onset from E3, E3b and E3c; memorization onset from E2, above the D2 floor.
  • Disclose thresholds. The pilot's coupled threshold |r| > 0.5 moved the boundary.
E5later

Jacobian rank of the trained denoiser (Hutchinson estimator)

Why

  • Model-side test of the subspace structure from C5.
  • Full SVD is infeasible at this size.

Terms in plain words

Cell
One map tile. One training example. Everything the model sees at once.
Unit
One pixel inside a cell: the patch of urban space that each indicator gives a score to.
Channel
One indicator's grid of unit scores for a cell. Six indicators make sixteen greyscale grids. The pilot's RGB file was three of these in an image container; the colours were never colours.
Tensor
The whole stack of grids for one cell. What the model learns from and what it generates.
Standardization
Shifting and scaling each channel so its values centre on zero with a spread of one. The shift and scale are stored, so the step can be undone on a generated sample.
Descriptor
A fixed recipe that turns one cell into a short list of numbers: per-channel average, spread, share of empty units, spatial clumping, class mix. Run on real and generated cells alike, then the two lists of numbers are compared.
F_mem, memorization fraction, k-NN gap ratio
The share of generated cells that are near-copies of a single training cell. Found by the gap ratio: distance to the nearest training cell divided by distance to the second nearest. A ratio below one third means the sample sits on top of one training cell and counts as a copy.
Noise floor
What F_mem reads on a model with random, never-trained weights. Only values above it mean the trained model actually copied.
Generalization window
The stretch of training after the model has learned the structure of the data and before it starts copying individual cells.
Generalization theory
Bonnaire et al.'s account of diffusion training: the model first learns the shared structure of the data and only later starts copying individual cells. The window between the two is what we study; memorization is its far edge.
Memorization onset, τ_mem
How many training steps pass before copying starts. Generalization theory shows it grows in proportion to the number of training cells, so bigger datasets stay in the window longer.
Intrinsic dimension
How many independent knobs it takes to describe the dataset, even though each cell holds millions of numbers. The pilot estimated about 33 by PCA.
PCA k95
The number of straight-line directions needed to explain 95 percent of the dataset's spread. Overestimates when the data is curved.
Levina-Bickel MLE
An estimate of intrinsic dimension from how nearest-neighbour distances grow with rank. It follows curvature, so it usually gives a smaller number than PCA.
Typology
A group of cells that look alike, found by clustering. The building blocks for dataset mutants.
HDBSCAN
A clustering method that finds groups of any shape, leaves stragglers unassigned, and copes with dense and sparse regions at the same time. Needs no preset number of clusters.
Subspace
A flat, low-dimensional sheet inside the big space of all possible cells. Wang et al. assume each typology lies on its own sheet.
k-center greedy
A way to pick a small set of cells that spread out over the whole dataset: repeatedly add the cell farthest from those already chosen.
Mutant
A training dataset made from the baseline by adding or removing whole typologies. Comparing models trained on mutants shows which structures survive.
Moran's I
A number for spatial clumping. Near 1, neighbouring units look alike; near 0, there is no spatial pattern at all.
Wasserstein distance
How much material you would have to move to turn one histogram into another. A distance between two distributions.
Jacobian rank
How many directions the trained denoiser actually responds to. A low rank means it has learned a low-dimensional structure rather than reacting to every pixel independently.
Hutchinson estimator
A trick to estimate that rank with random probes instead of computing the full matrix, which would be far too large.
UMAP and t-SNE
Two ways to flatten many-number cells onto a 2-D map so that cells which resemble each other land close together. Good for seeing clusters and gaps, bad for measuring: distances on the map are not distances in the data. UMAP can place new points on a map fitted earlier; t-SNE must be refitted from scratch.
Bivariate Moran's I
Moran's I between two grids: whether high values of one indicator sit next to high values of another in space. Near 1, buildings cluster around accessible units; near 0, the two grids ignore each other spatially.
Small multiples
Many small panels with the same layout side by side, one per channel, so the eye compares them.
False-colour composite
Showing any three channels as red, green and blue. What remote sensing does with satellite bands.
